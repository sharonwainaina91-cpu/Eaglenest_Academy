Welcome to Eaglenest Academy

Welcome to Eaglenest Academy, where we believe every child is unique and has unlimited potential. As we journey together in implementing the Competency-Based Curriculum, we focus on developing well-rounded learners who are not only academically excellent but also equipped with essential life skills.

Our dedicated team of experienced educators creates a nurturing environment where children thrive academically, socially, emotionally, and physically. We partner with parents to ensure each child receives personalized attention and support on their learning journey.

I invite you to visit our campus and experience firsthand the Eaglenest difference—where learning is joyful, purposeful, and transformative.

Dr. Margaret Wanjiku

Principal, Eaglenest Academy
